Stage 01 / 05

Flat · waiting

01 / 05 · Structure

Read the trend

Higher highs and higher lows on the hour chart. That means I only look for buys.

02 / 05 · Liquidity

Find the stops

Orders rest beyond swing highs and lows. The high above is my target. The low below is the bait.

03 / 05 · Sweep

Let it run

Price takes the low, triggers the stops, and snaps back. The rejection has to be fast, not slow.

04 / 05 · Return

Wait for the block

The last down candle before the sweep is the order block. Mark it on 5M, then watch the retrace on 1M.

05 / 05 · Execute

Take the trade

Entry at the block. Stop under the sweep with a few ticks of clearance. Target just under buy-side liquidity.

The rules

They don't move

1–3%

Risk per trade

Small losses keep me in the game.

On a $10,000 account that is $100 to $300 at risk, never more. I can take a long losing streak and still be here.

1:2

Minimum reward to risk

Win one in three and I break even.

Every winner pays twice what a loser costs, so a 50% hit rate is solidly profitable. I check it with the long or short position tool before entry.

3

Trades per day, max

Three, even if all three lose.

The cap is there to kill greed and revenge trading. The fourth idea waits for tomorrow.

+2%

Then I'm done

One solid winner ends the session.

A trade worth 2% or more of the account is the signal to stop. Don't give it back.
